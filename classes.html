Questions

Gym Questions

How much is gym membership at Semper Fit?

Ksh 11,000 for one school term, Ksh 30,000 for three terms (school holidays not included), or Ksh 1,500 for a day pass.

What time is the gym open?

Monday to Friday 5am-8am and 4pm-8pm, Saturday 6am-6pm and Sunday 10am-2pm.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa to Paybill 4122613, account St. Christophers Holdings-GYM.

Where is Semper Fit?

Inside St. Christopher's International School in Karen, Nairobi. The padel courts are reached through Gate 3a.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Sessions are one to one with a Semper Fit trainer. Message us on WhatsApp on 0742 822 357 to arrange one.

Is there padel too?

Yes. Semper Fit has three covered padel courts, open every day from 6am to 9pm, with 60 or 90 minute sessions. See padel.
