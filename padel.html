New to Padel?

Why Padel?

Padel is a racket sport played in pairs on a small court with glass walls. The ball can come back off the walls, so rallies last longer than in tennis and most people pick it up quickly.

Questions

Padel Questions

How much does it cost to hire a padel court at Semper Fit?

Ksh 3,000 for 60 minutes or Ksh 4,000 for 90 minutes, for the whole court. Up to four people play on one court.

When are the padel courts open?

Every day from 6am to 9pm.

Where are the courts?

At St. Christopher's International School in Karen, Nairobi. Come in through Gate 3a.

How do I book a court?

Book online on this page, or call 0720 720 444.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa to Paybill 4122613, account Padel.

I have never played. Can I still come?

Yes. Book a lesson with a coach, or bring friends and hire rackets and balls at the courts.

Are the courts covered?

Yes. All three courts are under a roof, with glass walls.
