Who We Are

A Gym and Three Courts

"Semper Fit" means "always fit". The idea is simple: turning up regularly matters more than anything else, so we try to make the gym a place you want to keep coming back to.

  • A gym floor with weights, machines and cardio
  • Boxing, HIIT and group classes with a coach
  • Personal training, one to one
  • Three covered padel courts, open every day
