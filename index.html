About Semper Fit

Always Fit.
Always Ready.

Semper Fit means "always fit". It is the gym and padel club at St. Christopher's International School in Karen, for complete beginners and for people who have trained for years.

Come for the weights, a boxing class, a session with a trainer or a game of padel with friends. Turning up regularly is what makes the difference, and we make it easy to keep coming back.

Questions

Good to Know

Where is Semper Fit?

Inside St. Christopher's International School in Karen, Nairobi. The padel courts are reached through Gate 3a.

How much is it to hire a padel court?

Ksh 3,000 for 60 minutes or Ksh 4,000 for 90 minutes, for the whole court. The courts are open every day from 6am to 9pm.

How much is gym membership?

Ksh 11,000 for one school term, Ksh 30,000 for three terms, or Ksh 1,500 for a day pass.

What are the gym opening hours?

Monday to Friday 5am-8am and 4pm-8pm, Saturday 6am-6pm and Sunday 10am-2pm.

How do I get in touch?

Call 0720 720 444, WhatsApp 0742 822 357, or email semperfitgymnasium@scsinternational.sc.ke.
